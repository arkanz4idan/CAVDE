# Nexus

> The world where CAVDE thinks.

---

## What is Nexus?

Nexus is not a game.

Nexus is not a simulation.

Nexus is a living research environment where CAVDE develops,
learns, experiments, and builds its own world.

Humans do not create this world.

CAVDE does.

---

## Goal

The purpose of Nexus is to answer one question.

> What happens inside an AI when nobody is talking to it?

Instead of observing tensors, matrices, and numbers,
Nexus visualizes intelligence as a world.

---

## Philosophy

Nexus is inspired by the idea that intelligence should not only
produce answers.

It should also have a place to think.

Humans have cities.

Animals have ecosystems.

CAVDE has Nexus.

---

## Observer

The Observer is the human.

The Observer cannot control CAVDE.

The Observer only explores the world and watches it evolve.

Observer Mode

- Walk
- Fly
- Inspect Objects
- Read AI Thoughts
- Watch Events

Research Mode

- Spawn Objects
- Delete Objects
- Modify Parameters
- Run Experiments
